Expand description

This rank’s share of the Liouvillian i[generator, operand], truncated by policy as one layer, where share is this rank’s share of the operand under rows. Collective.

Every rank passes the same generator, rows and policy; partner r ⊕ δ receives only the terms of share that anticommute with a generator term of partition delta δ, so a generator whose terms mostly keep their partition exchanges a halo rather than the share. Every rank’s share must carry the same bucket hash rows; bucket counts may differ and are raised to the group’s largest.

§Panics

If the ranks disagree about generator, rows or the bucket hash rows, if rows does not name one partition per rank, if the qubit counts differ, or if policy has a layer pass with no partitioned form.
