Operator Loschmidt-echo read-outs on a propagated operator A: the exact rotated overlap 2⁻ⁿ Tr(A V† A V) and the anticommutation histogram behind its diagonal approximation. V = ⊗_{q ∈ sites} exp(-iδ G_q) with G_q the single-qubit X or Z named by RotationAxis.
